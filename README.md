## Welcome To Womp's Shellfish Mod!

Have you ever wanted some shellfish in Minecraft, or even just some more aquatic life to brighten the experience of the game? If so, this is the mod for you!
Womp's Shellfish Mod is a project that adds several different shellfish, plants, blocks, food items, and more to the game to make your gameplay experience much more exciting.
<p></p>
Feel free to use this mod in modpacks on CurseForge!
